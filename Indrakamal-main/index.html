Message From CEO

Indrakamal Cooperative is a member-focused organization committed to enhancing financial stability and community growth. We provide tailored savings, credit, and investment solutions, promote financial discipline, and foster inclusivity. Beyond financial services, our cooperative nurtures community, transparency, and trust, empowering members to achieve financial independence while contributing to societal development. With over 25,000 satisfied members, we continue to be Nepal's most trusted cooperative society for secure financial solutions.

About Indrakamal Cooperative

Building Financial Futures Together Since 2005

For over 18 years, Indrakamal Multipurpose Cooperative Ltd. has been empowering communities across Nepal through innovative cooperative financial services. As one of Kathmandu's leading cooperative societies, we believe in putting our members first, offering personalized financial solutions that grow with your dreams. Our mission is to provide secure, accessible, and profitable financial services to every Nepali household.
